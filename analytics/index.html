About These Numbers

These are public, permanent records on Arweave. This site records the page name, selected button clicks, time, and a random session ID. It does not include names, email addresses, IP addresses, referrers, or full browsing URLs in its records. The network services receiving requests can still see connection information.

Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or when a tab closes. Counts are approximate: blocked tracking, quick exits, and indexing delays can leave activity out. Clicks do not confirm a download or a message. Public records can be submitted by others, so these are not audited visitor counts.

Local previews and this dashboard do not record visits. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected.
